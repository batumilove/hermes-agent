# Batumi Panorama — developer meeting brief

## Diagnosis
Batumi Panorama does not read like a live, well-managed project; it reads like a fragmented promise with no current owner of the truth. Public sources conflict on completion, unit count, finish level, and what is actually operating today. That confusion is now the story, and buyers will assume execution or liquidity problems unless the developer can prove otherwise. The meeting should test whether this is mainly a communication failure or a deeper delivery problem.

## Recommended posture
Be direct, document-focused, and hard to charm. Do not argue theory; ask for dated proof, exact numbers, and operational facts. If answers stay vague, treat that as the answer.

## 1. Construction reality
1. What is the exact current status of the project today: completed, partially completed, stalled, or being relaunched? I want a phase-by-phase breakdown, not a sales description.
2. Why do public sources show completion in 2019, construction updates ending around 2021, and 2026 resale listings still describing unfinished or white-frame inventory? Which version is true, and what evidence can you show on the spot?
3. How many total units actually exist today, and why do public portals variously describe 100 units, 300 apartments, or mixed apartment-plus-cottage counts? If your own product definition is unclear online, why should a buyer trust the rest?
4. What finish standards were actually delivered by unit type: black frame, white frame, or turnkey? I want a clean matrix of what was promised, what was built, and what remains incomplete.
5. Which amenities are physically operational right now—pool, spa, restaurant, shop, security, concierge, playground—and which are still planned? If something is not live, give the activation date and what is blocking it.

## 2. Marketing gaps
6. Why does the project still rely on stale aggregator pages instead of a current official source of truth? Who owns narrative control today?
7. If the project is healthy, where are the dated site photos, current walkthrough videos, resident activity, and occupancy proof? Right now the public footprint suggests silence, not momentum.
8. Why is the location still marketed in soft, elastic terms like “10–15 minutes to Batumi” and “5 minutes to the sea” without explaining hill access and transport friction? Are you selling reality or just angle-of-view copy?
9. What is the current buyer funnel, and at what point are prospects dropping out? If you know trust is the issue, what exactly have you changed in the last 90 days?

## 3. Trust repair
10. Why have contradictory claims about completion, inventory, and finish level been left uncorrected across public portals for years? Is this neglect, or is there no agreed internal version of the truth?
11. Who is prepared to stand behind the project publicly now: developer, operator, sales head, or asset manager? Buyers do not trust anonymous copy; they trust accountable names and documents.
12. What is the plain-English explanation for the gap between the original promise and the current market perception? If there were delays, partial delivery, or strategy changes, say so clearly instead of letting the market assume distress.
13. What proof can you show that units are actually occupied, rented, or being used successfully today? One live operating asset tells a stronger story than ten recycled brochures.
14. Resale listings are leaking into the market in a way that looks like owner exit or distressed inventory. What is driving that, and how are you preventing the secondary market from becoming the real public narrative?

## 4. Financials
15. On what evidence were claims like 4–5 year payback or 22% annual ROI based, and do those claims still stand today? If yes, show real operating numbers; if no, say they were marketing fiction.
16. What are the real current economics: sold units, unsold units, occupied units, average achieved rents, service costs, and management fees? I want actual performance, not projected yield language.
17. Is this fundamentally a sales problem, a trust problem, a completion problem, or a capital problem? Pick one as the primary constraint and explain what fixes it.
18. What financing or liquidity pressure exists at project level today? If more capital is needed to complete or activate the site properly, how much is needed and where does it come from?
19. What is the credible 12-month recovery plan from here—construction, operations, sales, and communications—and what milestones should an outsider hold you to? If there is no measurable plan, then there is no turnaround story.
